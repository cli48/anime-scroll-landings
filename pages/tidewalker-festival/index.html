The rhythm

The tide keeps the beat. You keep up.

No fixed countdown, no metronome — just the ocean's own push and pull. Learn a shoreline's pattern and you'll never lose your footing, even when the water climbs to your waist.

Collect wax, wicks, and rare tide-glass charms to customize your own lantern string. Rare colors only drift ashore on nights when the moon runs full.
